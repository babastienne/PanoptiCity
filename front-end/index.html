    Welcome to PanoptiCity

    This interactive map reveals the scale of mass surveillance worldwide. Each marker represents a known camera location and it's estimated field of view. The map displays a tiny fraction of the reality as the majority of cameras as not been yet contributed.

    • Zoom and pan to explore surveillance coverage
    • Toggle between different range scenarios
    • Sign in to contribute camera locations to OpenStreetMap

    Log in to add a camera

    You want to improve this map by contributing ? That's great ! The data used here is from the community website OpenStreetMap. To contribute to this awesome dataset you need an OpenStreetMap account (you can create one right now in 30 seconds).
